# Community

Downloads tell us the gem installs. They do **not** tell us what you are building.

Help us prioritize by sharing a use case — anonymized details are fine.

## Share what you're building

Post in **[Show and tell](https://github.com/Tiny-Bubble-Company/rails-agents/discussions/1)** with:

1. What agent(s) you built (one sentence each)
2. Provider / model (`:openai`, `:anthropic`, `:openrouter`, `:grok`)
3. Tools or skills you used
4. Biggest friction (optional)

Or open a structured issue: **[Share a use case](https://github.com/Tiny-Bubble-Company/rails-agents/issues/new?template=use_case.yml)**
